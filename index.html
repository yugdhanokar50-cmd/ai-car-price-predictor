1. The AI Concept (How to explain in Viva)

"This project uses a Rule-Based AI Expert System. Instead of training an opaque statistical Machine Learning model on a dataset, it uses an expert-curated Knowledge Base and a deterministic Inference Engine that applies heuristic IF-THEN rules to evaluate factors like vehicle age, odometer mileage, fuel efficiency, and ownership history."

4. Top Viva Questions & Answers

Q: Why is Rule-Based AI chosen over Machine Learning here?

A: Rule-based systems provide 100% explainability, require zero training data or GPUs, never overfit, and have deterministic results ideal for structured domain rules.

Q: Can the price ever drop below zero?

A: No. The algorithm enforces a safety residual floor of 8% of the base price to represent structural scrap and parts value.
